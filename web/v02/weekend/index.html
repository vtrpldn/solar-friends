    A trip to town

    Choose resident pickup or paid supplier delivery in Food. Surplus is reserved for a town trip at the displayed quote. Buyers pay less for additional batches and have daily limits. Transport is charged per trip.

    Connect the village

    Pipes need a pump and power at the source. River water must be filtered by a powered cistern. Greywater goes to reed beds; sewage goes to a biodigester or banana bed.
