# Farming and animal husbandry

## Why

Give village planning more choices about land, water, food, livestock feed and community work without replacing residents' leisure with constant chores.

## Implementation

- [ ] Expand garden crops with maize, cassava and fodder. Give rotations a visible soil benefit and retain the existing rain, local irrigation, compost and crop controls.
- [ ] Add a perennial orchard, a two-goat pasture and an apiary, with construction recipes, delivered planting/animal stock and consistent painted artwork.
- [ ] Keep fodder, milk and honey in actual local stores and carried shipments. Respect expiry, storage, production limits and material/water accounts.
- [ ] Let residents tend animals and collect products during community work hours. Require delivered feed and fresh drinking water; pause production when care or supplies are missing. Bees need nearby flowering plots.
- [ ] Integrate readable building controls, pantry and town supplies, Story introductions, Sandbox and a protected farming development scene.
- [ ] Check actual resident work, dry/wet conditions, overcrowding, moving/removal, invalid saves and exact save round trips. Play through the new controls and visually inspect every new sprite against existing buildings and terrain.
