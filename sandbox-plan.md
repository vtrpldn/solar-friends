# Sandbox simulation options

## Why

Players need to compare village layouts and resource choices without replaying Story unlocks. Free placement remains the default. Optional constraints use the same resident work, accounts and needs as Story.

- [ ] Add saved, independent construction/supplies, living-land and utility options. Story keeps all three constraints; Sandbox starts with all three off.
- [ ] Construction and supplies: new projects require delivered materials and resident labour; funds, food, tools and planting stock stop replenishing. Food keeps its real dates. Existing buildings, orders and ongoing work retain their ownership when options change.
- [ ] Living land: soil suitability, moisture and finite deposits affect growth and extraction when enabled. Turning it off preserves existing soil and deposit history while providing neutral growth and non-depleting extraction; enabling it resumes from the preserved state.
- [ ] Utilities: the enabled rule requires local water, separate waste handling and connected electrical supply. The comfortable rule supplies local fresh water, handles buffer overflow and provides explicit free operating power without changing owned battery energy or paid lines.
- [ ] Expose the options in the game settings with their current effects. Use normal saves and Continue sandbox; changes in protected scenes never affect the saved village. Artwork scenes explicitly select the constraints they demonstrate.
- [ ] Verify option independence, real construction/hauling and shortages, stock dates, land persistence, utility consumption and exact saved continuation. Review desktop controls and art in the running game.

Changes do not grant Story progress or retroactively charge free buildings. Pending resident work is retained when an option is disabled. Free operating power is reported separately from physical battery generation and consumption. Seasons, equipment wear, resident learning and supplier variation keep their existing independent options.

This is Stage 7 of [the simulator plan](simulator-plan.md). Outcome-based Story teaching and the paired housing-expansion balance remain required after these options.
