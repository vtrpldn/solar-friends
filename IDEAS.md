# Building ideas

These ideas are deferred while building placement is improved.

- Kitchen: choose quick meals that use fewer ingredients or shared dinners that take longer and improve friendships. Show residents carrying ingredients, cooking, and eating together.
- Chicken coop: feed hens and collect eggs. Hungry hens stop laying; eggs become kitchen ingredients.
- Homes: assign residents and add upgrades such as comfortable beds or a shaded porch. Improve rest and create places for neighbours to visit.

The proposed cycle is growing food, feeding hens, collecting eggs, cooking meals, and helping residents recover and connect. Start with coops and kitchens; add homes after the cycle feels fun.
