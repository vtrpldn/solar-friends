# Building ideas

The crop, animal-care and kitchen loops are implemented: residents carry supplies, tend animals, collect products and prepare saved cooking batches. Food freshness, local buffers, water, waste and optional biogas affect production. Existing work poses show cooking, watering, tending and feeding. These are delivered systems, not future features.

The following extensions remain ideas:

- Kitchen: let the player choose between a quick meal and a longer shared dinner with a friendship benefit. A dedicated group dining scene could make that choice visible.
- Animal care: add distinct egg-basket and feed-sack animations beyond the existing carrying and tending poses.
- Homes: assign residents explicitly and add upgrades such as comfortable beds or a shaded porch, with a visible comfort effect.

Finish the current [art, playtesting and performance pass](finishing-plan.md) before adding these extensions.
