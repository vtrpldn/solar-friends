# Village work and trade

## Why

The regular-game catalog playthrough exposed repetitive selling, construction competing with essential work, and bamboo dominating low-effort income. Players can now choose reserves and work allocation without overriding resident needs.

## Changes

Resources → Village work & trade contains per-resource automatic sales, minimum reserve, trigger threshold, and daily unit cap (zero means unlimited). Sales use existing whole-batch prices and the finance ledger. Food reserves and the next two batches of repeating production remain protected. Automatic sales are off by default and disabled in sandbox.

Each production site has a stock target, defaulting to 24. Pending output counts toward the target. Work priorities and reserved available workers keep services and production moving alongside construction. Needs still interrupt work; an assigned batch otherwise finishes before reassignment to avoid constant job switching.

Bamboo produces 8 per harvest instead of 12, with less hands-on work and longer regrowth. Greenhouses produce more food with more water and labor. Orchards, bananas, and honey have longer regrowth. Eggs and milk yield more per visit, with longer intervals. Prices and forestry recipes remain unchanged.

## Evidence and limits

`report-v02-economy.mjs` compares nine chains, including raw wood through timber and planks. The checked-in before snapshot and generated income report separate input costs, labor, land, capital and regrowth. These are theoretical estimates, not observed earnings; travel, needs, water infrastructure, power and sale batch rounding reduce actual returns.

`verify-v02-economy-policies.mjs` checks reserves, caps and day rollover, ledger entries, save/load, invalid settings, concurrent construction/production and aggregate stock targets.

`verify-v02-economy-soak.mjs` replays the genuine Day 61 normal-game save for three game days. It places and pays for a shared house through the normal command and adds no money, materials or instant construction. The house completed, cash grew from R$16.02 after ordering to R$1,288.02, sampled food/water/cash remained positive, and the end save restored. This is automated simulation evidence, not another manual browser playthrough or proof of universal balance across seeds.

Urgent-needs, playtest-regression and lab fixed-step checks pass. Desktop browser checks covered reserve edits, activation and layout. Further longer playthroughs should assess whether greenhouse returns or livestock capital costs need another pass.
