# Native variation and construction studies

## Why

Repeated mature models make a biome feel planted in rows. The shared low-poly recipes now supply alternate silhouettes, juvenile plants and visible building work stages without introducing a separate art style.

The collection contains 223 models, adding 118 to the prior 105:

- 18 mature variations across nine existing native tree/cactus families.
- 18 sapling/young stages, with simple branching and no mature fruit; young Juçara uses paired leaflets and Embaúba uses palmate leaf clusters.
- Eight fern, bromeliad, macambira and grass patches.
- 18 alternate crop models across six crops and their three existing stages.
- 56 groundwork/assembly props covering all 28 homes/buildings. Framed structures, masonry tanks, beds, pasture fences, solar rails, streetlights, apiaries and the river generator have separate construction treatments.

These are selectable art states, not timed construction or growth simulation. Stage props retain the finished building's footprint. Native variants reuse the species already represented in the project; this does not claim that every represented species is exclusive to its biome.

Biome studies choose from mature variations and young stages. The gallery uses 15 columns at four-cell intervals so all 223 props fit without overlapping footprints. Existing saved scenes keep their placements; Restore gallery loads the expanded lineup.

Validation: all catalog models pass bounds, compaction, polygon budgets and scale checks. Growth checks cover nine increasing tree-height sequences, six alternate crop sequences, construction footprints and non-overlapping gallery placements.
