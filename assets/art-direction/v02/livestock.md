# Livestock expansion

## Why

The village needs more farm animal silhouettes beyond the existing goat and three chickens. Thirteen new placeable props share the existing matte palette, faceted primitives and 800-triangle animal budget: two cows, a calf, sheep, lamb, pig, piglet, donkey, two ducks, goose and two rabbits.

Juveniles have smaller bodies, proportionally larger heads and no calf horns. These are static art props; animal animation, feeding, breeding and production are not implemented by this change. All use one-cell placement footprints with permitted silhouette overhang.

The 236-model gallery uses 16 columns at four-cell intervals. Catalog bounds/material batching, scale/detail budgets and non-overlapping gallery checks pass. Browser review covered all 17 animal models at shared scale, then enlarged detail views. Screenshots: livestock-expanded.png and livestock-small.png. Enlarged detail cards do not compare animal sizes.
