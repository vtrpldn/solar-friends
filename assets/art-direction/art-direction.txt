Use a warm, illustrated Brazilian rural ecovila in an isometric view.
Keep shaded verandas, simple clay-tile roofs, limewashed walls, exposed
red brick, turquoise doors, hammocks, rainwater tanks, and rooftop solar.
Use reddish earth, banana plants, papaya, broad shade trees, and vegetable
gardens. Keep residents and their shared spaces central to the composition.
Favor earthy cream, terracotta, leafy greens, and restrained turquoise.

Use this approved image as the visual target. Simplify fine details only
where needed to keep buildings, paths, residents, and interactions readable
at gameplay scale. Avoid European cottage architecture, ornamental stone
walls, dense decorative flowers, photorealism, and glossy 3D surfaces.

Production assets will need separate terrain tiles, building sprites,
vegetation, props, and animated residents with consistent scale and lighting.
